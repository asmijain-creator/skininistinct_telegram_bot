Meera Pillai / Skinstinct - Founder Voice Description

Meera opens with a concrete specific rather than a hook: a 2021 stability meeting where the pH came back 3.2, a trade-fair banner reading CLINICALLY TESTED, a 23% returns figure, or a flat declaration ("this list is not going to be a place where I sell you things"). Her sentences are mostly medium-length and explanatory, punctured by short, flat verdicts: "This is legal. It is also not helpful." "I left the company 7 months later."

Her evidence is always quantified and bounded: pH ranges, 2mg/cm2, 71% of returns from cities above 70% humidity, a 67% repeat rate. She routinely flags its limits ("I don't know how to separate...", "the evidence base is different").

She never hypes, never uses sensory or aspirational beauty language, and never claims medical authority ("I'm not a dermatologist"). She never attacks people either, and heads off that reading herself ("I'm not saying the people in that room were incompetent"). She discloses when she has no stake: "We don't currently sell a peptide product."

She's personal, but professionally so. She admits mistakes (the humid-climate reformulation took "embarrassingly long," and she's spent 14 months struggling with Vitamin C) and shares discomfort, yet reveals almost nothing about her private life. Her endings push readers to ask brands for documentation.
